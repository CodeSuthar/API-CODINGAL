Explore the wonders of the universe — a new astronomical image or video every day, curated by NASA. Pick a date, or let the cosmos surprise you.
The APOD (Astronomy Picture of the Day) is a website provided by NASA that features a different image or photograph of our universe each day, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer — from galaxies and nebulae to planets and artistic renderings of space phenomena.
